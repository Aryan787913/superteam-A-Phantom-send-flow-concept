## Five Newcomer Problems
1. **Assumption, grounded in designer-reported observation:** Send listed only Cash in a new empty wallet. A newcomer may not understand why SOL is absent. Proposal: explain Cash versus SOL and give an explicit funding path. This is not evidence that Phantom never supports SOL sends.
2. **Assumption, grounded in designer-reported observation:** no Swap option was visible in the tested empty-wallet state. A newcomer may not know their next step. Do not claim Swap is absent from the product.
3. **Assumption, grounded in designer-reported observation:** History was empty. An empty history cannot establish whether spam filtering is present or absent. Proposal: explain that trusted contacts are distinct from transaction senders.
4. **Assumption:** users accustomed to UPI names may trust matching address prefixes and suffixes. Proposal: show the full address and compare the middle against saved contacts. Validate with Task B; screenshot evidence cannot establish this behavior.
5. **Assumption:** SOL-denominated amounts and fee errors may confuse INR-first users. Proposal: INR-first amount, approximate fiat fee, plain-language failure and recovery. Validate with Task A.

## Existing Protections And Limits
Phantom already has transaction history, P&L, simulation previews, a malicious-site blocklist and spam-token hiding. The proposal adds a contact-aware recipient check and clearer empty-wallet/fee language; it does not claim to invent those protections. Prefix/suffix matching only catches one pattern. Saved contacts can be incorrect or compromised. Never promise scam-proof transfers.
